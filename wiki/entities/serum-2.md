# Serum 2

Serum 2 is an advanced virtual synthesizer instrument plug-in by [[entities/xfer-records]]. The manual identifies it as a plug-in for Microsoft Windows and Apple macOS, supporting VST3, Audio Unit, and AAX hosts.

## Product role

Serum 2 combines multiple synthesis methods into a single instrument:

- Wavetable synthesis.
- Subtractive/filter-based shaping.
- Multisampled instruments.
- Sample instruments.
- Granular synthesis.
- Spectral synthesis.
- Sub oscillator and noise oscillator support.
- Deep modulation.
- Integrated FX.
- Clips and arpeggiator.
- Wavetable editing/importing.

## Why it matters

Serum 2 is useful because it is not locked to one synthesis model. It can function as a wavetable synth, sampler-like instrument, granular texture engine, spectral resynthesizer, FX-heavy sound-design environment, or patch-contained performance system.
