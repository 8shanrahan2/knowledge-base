# Xfer Records

Xfer Records is the organization behind [[entities/serum-2]]. The Serum 2 User Guide points users to Xfer Records for account access, downloads, registration, updates, and support.

## Role in the manual

The manual references Xfer Records in these contexts:

- Serum 2 product ownership and copyright.
- Downloading installers through the Xfer Records account page.
- Registration and update access.
- Support contact through the Xfer Records support site.
- Product file/install workflows.
