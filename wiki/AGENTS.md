# Wiki Maintenance Instructions

This wiki follows an OpenKB-style layout.

## Page types

- `summaries/` — source-document summaries.
- `concepts/` — reusable ideas extracted from the source.
- `entities/` — products, organizations, people, places, or named systems.
- `explorations/` — applied synthesis, workflows, Q&A, and practical guides.
- `index.md` — central graph entry point.

## Style rules

- Prefer durable concepts over one-off notes.
- Use Obsidian-style `[[wikilinks]]` for cross-references.
- Keep pages grounded in source documents.
- Split pages when a concept has multiple independent workflows.
- Add practical examples when they make the manual easier to use.
- Avoid copying long passages from the source manual; summarize and point to concepts.
