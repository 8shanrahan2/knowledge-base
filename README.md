## What this wiki is for

The goal is to turn a long PDF manual into durable working knowledge: source summaries, concept pages, entity pages, workflow explorations, and cross-links. Instead of repeatedly asking an assistant to reread the whole manual, the wiki becomes a persistent layer of summarized and connected knowledge.
